Quick Guide

How to Play

01 🕹️

Move & Wobble

Use Arrow Keys or WASD to slide, squish, and bounce around the oak tabletop.

02 🫧

Pop Water Bubbles

Aim with your mouse and press Spacebar or Left Click to pop bubbles at dust bunnies.

03 🛡️

100 HP & 50 HP Revive

100 Health Points! Avoid enemies when health drops to 50 or below to trigger emergency revive!

04 🤝

Invite a Friend

Click Multiplayer, generate a room code like SLIME-XXXX, and share it with a friend to explore together!
